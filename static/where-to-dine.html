From Our Table to Yours

Cafe da Cunha

Mentioned earlier Cafe is one of the few places available for dining.

Until March 2013 was known as "The Coffee Shop" and in fact they were selling the tea, other refreshments and some hot meals after opening in 2010.

All the special events are held right here.

The Albatross Bar and Cafe

Second place that you as a tourist should visit and take into account.

The cafe serves beer and other locally brewed drinks, but worry not if you're not drinking alcohol - the cafe also serves tea and coffee by the day.

A warm and cozy place to spend your evening in.

Opening Hours: Monday-Saturday: 14:30-02:30 | Sunday: 11:35-20:25

Dinning with the Locals

It would be unforgivable mistake not to mention the possibility to share a meal with the local folks.

The islanders are famous for their hospitality and kindness, so if you're staying at one's home - be sure that they won't leave you without a food.
